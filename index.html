About Me

The planner behind NVE

I started NVE because I believe a celebration should feel like a work of art: considered in its proportions, generous in its details and calm at its centre.

My approach draws on the Renaissance masters and on Roman architecture. Both prized proportion, restraint and craft, and both understood that what is left out shapes a space as much as what is put in. I bring the same thinking to guest lists, floor plans and timelines.

Working with me means one point of contact, clear communication and a plan that holds together on the day.

Guiding principles

  1. I

    Proportion

    Every element earns its place and sits in right relation to the others.

  2. II

  3. III

    Craft

    Details are made and checked with care, long before the day arrives.
